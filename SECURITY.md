# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately by emailing
[hello@thalovant.com](mailto:hello@thalovant.com).

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Include the affected
repository or package, the observed behavior, and the minimum reproduction
details needed to investigate. Do not include real credentials, customer data,
or private exploit details in public channels.

The maintainers will acknowledge critical reports within one hour and high
severity reports within one business day. The remediation targets are 24 hours
for critical findings and seven days for high findings.

This organization-level policy applies to Thalovant repositories that do not
contain a more specific local `SECURITY.md`.
