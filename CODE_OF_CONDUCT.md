# Code of Conduct

Thalovant's public spaces (repositories, issues, discussions and the Discord)
are for people building and using voice and assistant software together. We want
them to be welcoming, practical and calm.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when you are unsure.
- Give useful, specific feedback, and accept it the same way.
- Respect others' privacy: never share someone's personal information, private
  messages or credentials.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or deliberately disruptive posts.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Write to [hello@thalovant.com](mailto:hello@thalovant.com) with a link or a
description. Reports are read by the maintainers and handled privately. We may
edit or remove content, lock a conversation, or restrict someone's participation
when this code is not followed. We will tell the reporter what was decided where
that is safe to do.

This code applies in all Thalovant spaces and when someone represents the
project in public.
